The path / 01

Development, proven over time.

NextPhaze is led by former Western Michigan University wide receiver #15 Carrington Thompson, part of the Broncos’ historic 2016 MAC Championship and Cotton Bowl team.

Before Western Michigan, WMU records credit Carrington with 89 receptions, 1,210 yards and 10 touchdowns across two seasons at Northwood University. At Western Michigan, he developed into a starting receiver and finished 2016 with 42 catches, 637 yards and six touchdowns.

After college, Carrington continued competing with the West Michigan Ironmen. That path—from one competitive phase to the next—is the authentic foundation of NextPhaze.

Championship production / 04

The work transferred.

In 2018 Carrington helped the West Michigan Ironmen win the MPIF championship, catching five touchdown passes in the title game. He was later identified as the league’s 2018 Offensive Rookie of the Year.

A 2019 FOX 17 profile also documented his leadership, connection with fans and interest in working with young people—a bridge between the player he was and the coach he is becoming through NextPhaze.
