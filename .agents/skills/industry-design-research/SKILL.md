# industry-design-research

## Activation

Use when the project needs category conventions, competitive patterns, trust barriers, or differentiation opportunities.

## Purpose

Load only the relevant industry intelligence and compare it with current competitors.

## Required inputs

- Completed project brief
- Industry selection
- Competitor or inspiration URLs

## Workflow

1. Read the matching industry document.
2. Separate user expectations from visual clichés.
3. Analyze references by principle, not surface imitation.
4. Identify required content, proof, IA, conversion, and structured-data patterns.
5. Write differentiation opportunities.

## Output

Industry brief with conventions, risks, opportunities, and prohibited copying targets.

## References

- `research/industry/*.md`
- `research/methodology.md`

## Scripts and tools

Use repository-native tooling first. Applicable tools may include browser screenshots, Playwright, axe-core, Lighthouse, HTML validation, schema validation, link checking, and asset-budget scripts. Do not claim a test ran unless evidence was produced.

## Failure conditions

- Industry is ambiguous
- References cannot be accessed or verified

## Quality checks

- [ ] At least three reference categories
- [ ] Transferable lessons and do-not-copy notes
- [ ] Recommendations tied to business context

- `design-discovery`
