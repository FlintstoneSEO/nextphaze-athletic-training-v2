# Art directions

All directions use the supplied NextPhaze logo, verified content, black/metallic-gold/white identity, and authentic Carrington imagery. They differ structurally—not merely by palette or type.

## A. Championship Editorial

Strategy: earn parent trust through sports-journal clarity while giving athletes the emotional force of a game-day feature.

- Composition: asymmetric two-column opening; image crops break the editorial grid; long horizontal credibility band; numbered section folios; stat spreads rather than cards.
- Content emphasis: athlete value first, Carrington proof second, programs and price third.
- Image behavior: large directional crop of the supplied Ironmen action shot; portrait used as a human counterpoint; duotone/gold overlays avoided so the images remain authentic.
- Density/component grammar: alternating dense fact bands and generous warm-white reading space; sharp rules, square corners, captions, scoreline typography.
- Type: wide, forceful display face paired with highly readable system sans; uppercase reserved for labels and stats.
- Interaction: restrained line reveals, ticker-like credibility motion only when motion is allowed, clear underlined text links.
- Mobile: opening becomes image-led but keeps the H1 and booking action in the first viewport; credibility becomes a readable wrapped list, not a marquee dependency; stat spreads recompose into two-column scoreboards.
- Accessibility/performance: high contrast, no text burned into images, limited motion, supplied images sized explicitly.
- Risk: can overemphasize Carrington's résumé if editorial proof is not balanced with training outcomes.

## B. Training Lab

Strategy: communicate precision, repeatability, and purposeful athlete development.

- Composition: dark full-width measurement grid, modular drill lanes, calibration marks, sticky progress rail, compact program selector.
- Content emphasis: training methods and movement categories before biography.
- Image behavior: action imagery clipped into narrow diagnostic frames with caption coordinates and motion vectors.
- Density/component grammar: technical, data-dense modules; thin borders; monospaced labels; route/timing diagrams as functional separators.
- Type: condensed performance display with monospaced measurement labels and neutral sans body copy.
- Interaction: selectable training focus updates a detail panel; progress indicators behave like testing stations.
- Mobile: horizontal drill lanes become a stepper; technical labels simplify; diagrams are decorative and hidden from assistive technology.
- Accessibility/performance: strongest contrast but greatest cognitive-density risk; interactive modules require more JavaScript and testing.
- Risk: may imply scientific measurement, testing equipment, or certifications that are not verified.

## C. Journey / Next Phase

Strategy: make progression the entire narrative system—from Carrington's path to the athlete's next phase.

- Composition: chronological opening, continuous phase line through milestones, diagonal transitions, chapter-based pages, booking as the final milestone.
- Content emphasis: Carrington's journey and philosophy lead; programs appear as the visitor's next chapter.
- Image behavior: documentary crops aligned to timeline milestones; missing historical stages need placeholders.
- Density/component grammar: milestone blocks, dates, chapter numbers, transition arrows, more vertical storytelling.
- Type: editorial serif/sans contrast, quieter stats, narrative captions.
- Interaction: scroll-progress timeline and chapter transitions.
- Mobile: a single vertical route with strong chapter markers; logical and readable but longer to reach price/booking.
- Accessibility/performance: semantic ordered list works well; scroll effects must be nonessential.
- Risk: available media cannot visually support every career phase, and the homepage could become a football biography.

## Agent-selected recommended direction

**Championship Editorial with a restrained progression line from Journey / Next Phase.** This is an agent recommendation for implementation under the autonomous-run clause, not client approval.

Rationale: it makes best use of the limited but strong authentic imagery, provides immediate training/pricing/booking clarity, and lets Carrington's verified career behave as evidence rather than the entire story. A subtle gold phase line and numbered chapter markers make “NextPhaze” structural without forcing a media-poor chronological homepage. Training Lab is rejected as the main system because its measurement language could imply testing capabilities not established in the brief. Journey is retained for the About timeline but not as the dominant homepage composition.
