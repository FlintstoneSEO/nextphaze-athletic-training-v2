# Athletic-performance website research

Research date: 2026-08-30. This is principle-level research; no competitor composition, copy sequence, artwork, or interaction is to be reproduced.

## Audience expectations

Parents need fast answers to five questions: what is trained, whether the coach is credible, who the training fits, what it costs, and how booking works. Athletes need visible competitive energy and a believable path of progression. Both audiences benefit from concrete program distinctions and real imagery more than inflated motivation language.

## Three reference categories

### Multi-location performance systems

Examples reviewed: [Parisi Speed School](https://www.parisischool.com/) and its [Tri-Cities location](https://parisispeed.com/).

- Transferable lesson: make athlete fit and the next action explicit early; organize training around outcomes parents understand.
- Category convention: large proof counts, proprietary systems, age-banded programs, and evaluation-first funnels.
- Do not copy: network-scale statistics, certification claims, age bands, results, or evaluation requirements. NextPhaze has not verified any of these.
- Opportunity: NextPhaze can feel more personal and credible through Carrington's specific progression story and transparent per-session prices.

### Elite sports academies

Example reviewed: [IMG Academy Athletics](https://athletics.imgacademy.com/home).

- Transferable lesson: training is more persuasive when framed as an integrated athlete-development system, not a list of drills.
- Category convention: cinematic media, broad performance disciplines, facility scale, consultant-led conversion.
- Do not copy: institutional scale, medicine/nutrition/recovery claims, campus language, or high-production media behavior unsupported by the available assets.
- Opportunity: use selective editorial photography and a concise development philosophy instead of simulating an academy Carrington does not operate.

### Position-specific and independent trainers

Observed pattern across current search results: coach-first identity, football-action imagery, social-video feeds, motivational headlines, and DM/text-led booking.

- Transferable lesson: real coach visibility and direct contact reduce trust friction.
- Common risk: generic black/red templates, unreadable condensed type, unverified outcome claims, and booking flows that hide price until late.
- Opportunity: lead with the athlete's development need, place Carrington's proof beside—not ahead of—the offer, publish exact verified prices, and make logistics unknowns honest.

## Required category conventions for NextPhaze

- Authentic coach imagery and clearly attributed career proof
- Plain-language service descriptions
- Group vs. one-on-one comparison before personal-data collection
- Price and availability visible before conversion
- Parent/guardian contact fields for athlete bookings
- Phone/email fallback while scheduling integration is pending
- Strong mobile navigation and large touch targets

## Differentiation strategy

NextPhaze should own “progression” as both narrative and interface grammar. A vertical phase line, route-step marks, numbered performance moments, and section transitions can connect Carrington's career progression with the athlete's next step. This creates an identity that remains recognizable without the logo and avoids the category's interchangeable gym-template aesthetic.

## Trust barriers and responses

| Barrier | Response |
| --- | --- |
| “Is this coach qualified?” | Verified WMU #15, MAC Championship, Cotton Bowl, and Ironmen proof with source links |
| “Is this right for my athlete?” | Outcome-led training descriptions and sport/goal intake; no unverified age claims |
| “What does it cost?” | $30 group / $60 one-on-one shown early and consistently |
| “Where and how long?” | Explicitly unresolved in documentation; booking confirmation language avoids guessing |
| “Can I book now?” | Clear booking request UI plus email/phone fallback; live-system status is honest |

## Prohibited copying targets

Do not reproduce Parisi's program taxonomy, IMG's ecosystem framing, any competitor testimonials or proof counts, or the identifiable layout/copy sequence of any reviewed site.
